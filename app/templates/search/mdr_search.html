Manorial Documents Register

The Manorial Documents Register (MDR) is the official index of records created by manors in England and Wales up until 1926.

What are the manorial records?

Manorial records are the administrative documents created by manors. The MDR details those created from the 13th century to the early-20th century in England and Wales.

Under the Manorial Documents Rules, these include

  • court rolls: formal record of the manor court, whether in roll-form or on sheets of paper
  • surveys: a written description of a manor consisting of descriptions of boundaries, customs and a rent roll
  • maps and terriers: maps and plans of a manor and a description
  • documents and books of every description relating to the boundaries, franchises, wastes, customs or courts of a manor

With the following exceptions:

  • it does not include title deeds (a legal document that proves who owns a specific piece of land or property)
  • it does not include documents created after 1925 (unless the manor court continued to meet after this date)
